Klaviyo and ActiveCampaign are both premium email tools, but they target fundamentally different use cases. Klaviyo is purpose-built for ecommerce (Shopify specifically). ActiveCampaign is purpose-built for B2B services. After tracking 4 Klaviyo accounts and 6 ActiveCampaign accounts from March to June 2026, here's the comparison.

## When Klaviyo wins over ActiveCampaign

- **You run a Shopify store doing $100K+/year** — Klaviyo's predictive analytics (LTV, churn risk) genuinely pay back
- **You want best-in-class ecommerce automations** — browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase flows
- **You send SMS + email** — Klaviyo's bundled SMS pricing is cheaper than ActiveCampaign's
- **You want native Shopify integration** — Klaviyo's app is best-in-class

## When ActiveCampaign wins over Klaviyo

- **You run a B2B service** (consulting, SaaS, agency) — ActiveCampaign's CRM + lead scoring pipeline is built for B2B sales
- **You want marketing + sales CRM in one platform** — saves $25-90/user/mo vs separate Salesforce
- **You need deep conditional automations** — branching, splits, goals for B2B nurture flows

## The verdict

For ecommerce stores, **Klaviyo is the better pick**. For B2B services, **ActiveCampaign is the better pick**. The two tools target different segments and shouldn't both be in your stack.

## Frequently asked questions about Klaviyo vs ActiveCampaign

### Which is better for ecommerce stores in 2026, Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign?
Klaviyo wins for ecommerce stores doing **$100K+/year**, especially on Shopify. Klaviyo's predictive analytics (LTV, churn risk, expected purchase date) and best-in-class Shopify integration pay back the pricing premium. At 1,000 contacts Klaviyo Email is **$45/mo**; Klaviyo Email + SMS is $60/mo. ActiveCampaign Plus at the same 1,000 contacts is **$49/mo** but lacks native Shopify sync (Shopify integration via Zapier only). For Shopify stores doing $100K-$2M/year, Klaviyo is the right pick.

### Which is better for B2B services in 2026, Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign?
ActiveCampaign wins for B2B services (consulting, SaaS, agencies). ActiveCampaign Plus includes **CRM + lead scoring + sales pipeline** at $49/mo for 1,000 contacts — replacing $25-90/user/mo Salesforce seats. Klaviyo's toolset is built around purchase data, so half its features (browse abandonment, predictive CLV) don't apply to B2B. ActiveCampaign's conditional branching, splits, and goals are built for B2B nurture flows. For B2B SaaS with sales teams, ActiveCampaign is the right pick.

### How does Klaviyo pricing compare to ActiveCampaign at the same subscriber count in 2026?
At 1,000 contacts Klaviyo Email is **$45/mo** vs ActiveCampaign Plus at **$49/mo** (ActiveCampaign wins by $4/mo). At 5,000 contacts Klaviyo is **$135/mo** vs ActiveCampaign Plus at $49/mo for 1K + tier-jump pricing above. At 10,000+ contacts Klaviyo Email scales linearly ($200/mo for 10K) while ActiveCampaign tiers jump steeper. Klaviyo's pricing advantage is at higher contact counts; ActiveCampaign's advantage is at lower contact counts where the bundled CRM offsets higher base price.

### Which has better SMS pricing in 2026, Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign?
Klaviyo has cheaper bundled SMS — Klaviyo Email + SMS includes **1,000 SMS messages/month at $60/mo** with overage at $0.01-$0.02/message. ActiveCampaign charges separately for SMS via ActiveCampaign SMS or third-party integrations (Twilio), and its SMS pricing is not bundled into Plus tier. For stores doing SMS + email from one platform, Klaviyo's bundle is the cheaper path; ActiveCampaign users typically add Twilio separately ($20/mo for 1K SMS).

### Does Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign have deeper automation in 2026?
ActiveCampaign has **deeper conditional branching** for B2B nurture flows — unlimited branching depth vs Klaviyo's more linear automation paths. ActiveCampaign Plus includes webhooks, lead scoring, and split testing on Plus tier ($49/mo); Klaviyo reserves predictive features for paid tiers ($45+/mo). However, for ecommerce-specific automation (browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase flows with personalized product recommendations), Klaviyo's automations are best-in-class. The choice depends on whether you need B2B branching depth or ecommerce flow polish.

### Can I use Klaviyo and ActiveCampaign together in 2026?
The two tools **target different segments and shouldn't both be in your stack** — combining them adds cost without meaningful benefit. Klaviyo handles ecommerce (Shopify sync, predictive LTV, browse abandonment); ActiveCampaign handles B2B (CRM, lead scoring, sales pipeline). For most businesses the choice is binary based on primary use case. The only scenario where both make sense is a brand running both ecommerce and B2B sales motions, which is rare.

### When should a Shopify store pick ActiveCampaign over Klaviyo in 2026?
Pick ActiveCampaign over Klaviyo **if your Shopify store also runs B2B sales motions (wholesale accounts, custom orders), you need lead scoring and a sales pipeline that Klaviyo doesn't have, you want deeper conditional branching for complex nurture flows, or your average order value is high enough to justify a CRM-led sales process**. For pure Shopify DTC ecommerce doing $100K+/year, Klaviyo wins on Shopify-specific features. For hybrid B2B + B2C Shopify, ActiveCampaign's CRM offsets Klaviyo's ecommerce depth.
