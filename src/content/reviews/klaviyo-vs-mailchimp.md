Klaviyo and Mailchimp are both popular, but they're built for fundamentally different use cases. Klaviyo is purpose-built for ecommerce — Shopify stores specifically. Mailchimp is general-purpose — multi-channel marketing. After tracking 4 Klaviyo accounts and 12 Mailchimp accounts from March to June 2026, here's what we found.

## At a glance: Klaviyo vs Mailchimp

| Feature | Klaviyo | Mailchimp |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Free tier | 250 contacts (free for 1K emails) | 500 contacts (Essentials-style) |
| Entry paid tier | $60/mo Email+SMS for 1K | $13/mo Essentials |
| 5K contacts cost | $135/mo | $135+/mo Standard |
| Shopify integration | First-class, native | Third-party plugins |
| Predictive analytics (LTV, churn) | ✅ Built-in | ❌ Not available |
| SMS bundling | ✅ Email + SMS bundle | SMS additional per message |
| Multi-channel (social, postcards) | ⚠️ Email + SMS only | ✅ Email + SMS + social + postcards + landing pages |

For **Email-only**, Klaviyo is more expensive than Mailchimp at small lists. Klaviyo's pricing advantage is **Email + SMS bundle** — Mailchimp charges separately per SMS.

## When Klaviyo wins over Mailchimp

- **You run a Shopify store doing $100K+/year** — Klaviyo's predictive analytics (LTV, churn risk) genuinely pay back
- **You send SMS in addition to email** — Klaviyo's bundled SMS pricing is cheaper
- **You need ecommerce-specific flows** (browse abandonment, cart abandonment with personalized product recommendations) — Klaviyo's are first-class
- **You want native Shopify integration** — Klaviyo's Shopify app is best-in-class

## When Mailchimp wins over Klaviyo

- **You're not an ecommerce store** — Mailchimp's newsletter focus is more relevant
- **You run multi-channel campaigns** (social, postcards, landing pages) — Mailchimp's broader features are more mature
- **You have <1,000 contacts** — Klaviyo at $45/mo is overkill for small lists
- **You need strong deliverability tracking** — Mailchimp's reporting is more detailed

## The verdict

For ecommerce stores doing $100K+/year, **Klaviyo is the better pick** — the predictive analytics and Shopify integration pay back the pricing premium. For non-ecommerce small businesses, **Mailchimp is the better pick** on price and feature breadth.

## Frequently asked questions about Klaviyo vs Mailchimp

### Which is cheaper for ecommerce in 2026, Klaviyo or Mailchimp?
At 1,000 contacts, Klaviyo Email is **$45/mo** vs Mailchimp Essentials at **$13/mo** — Mailchimp wins by $32/mo at small lists. At 5,000 contacts Klaviyo Email is **$135/mo** vs Mailchimp Standard at **$135+/mo** (basically tied). At 10,000 contacts both are $250/mo. The pricing flip: Klaviyo is more expensive than Mailchimp for email-only at small lists, but **Klaviyo's Email + SMS bundle at $60/mo is cheaper than Mailchimp's separate SMS charges** plus email. The decision boils down to whether you need SMS bundled or not.

### Which has better Shopify integration in 2026, Klaviyo or Mailchimp?
Klaviyo has **first-class native Shopify integration** with real-time sync of products, customers, orders, abandoned carts, and browse history. Mailchimp's Shopify integration is via **third-party plugins** (Mailchimp for Shopify app), which is functional but shallower. Klaviyo's predictive analytics (LTV, churn risk, expected purchase date) are powered by Shopify data that Mailchimp cannot match. For serious Shopify stores doing $100K+/year, Klaviyo's integration depth is the deciding factor over Mailchimp.

### Does Klaviyo or Mailchimp have predictive analytics in 2026?
Klaviyo includes predictive analytics (predicted LTV, churn risk, next order date) **on lower tiers starting at $45/mo Email plan**. Mailchimp does **not offer predictive analytics on any tier** — its reporting focuses on opens, clicks, conversions, and revenue attribution but lacks LTV and churn predictions. For ecommerce stores that want to segment by predicted customer value or churn risk, Klaviyo is the only option between the two; Mailchimp users who need this level of predictive segmentation typically add a separate tool like Segment or BigCommerce's built-in analytics.

### Which has better multi-channel marketing features in 2026, Klaviyo or Mailchimp?
Mailchimp wins for multi-channel breadth — Mailchimp Standard and Premium include **email + SMS + social posting + postcards + landing pages + brand kit** in one platform. Klaviyo focuses on email + SMS only, without social posting, postcards, or multi-channel campaign management. For brands running multi-channel campaigns (Instagram + email + SMS + direct mail postcards), Mailchimp's broader feature set wins. For pure ecommerce email + SMS automations, Klaviyo's depth wins.

### Does Klaviyo or Mailchimp have a better free tier in 2026?
Mailchimp's free tier covers **500 contacts (Essentials-style, basic features)** vs Klaviyo's free tier at **250 contacts with 500 email sends/month**. Mailchimp has 2x the free-tier subscriber cap, but Klaviyo's free tier includes real ecommerce automation (Shopify sync, basic segmentation for purchase history). For brand-new Shopify stores testing ecommerce automation, Klaviyo's free tier is more useful; for non-ecommerce newsletters testing multi-channel features, Mailchimp's free tier is better.

### When should an ecommerce store pick Mailchimp over Klaviyo in 2026?
Pick Mailchimp over Klaviyo **if you run multi-channel campaigns (email + social + postcards + landing pages) and want one platform, have <1,000 contacts where Mailchimp's $13/mo Essentials beats Klaviyo's $45/mo, want stronger deliverability tracking and reporting depth, or run a non-Shopify store where Klaviyo's ecommerce depth is wasted**. Mailchimp also wins for B2B newsletters and content publishers that don't need ecommerce-specific features. For pure Shopify DTC stores doing $100K+/year, Klaviyo is the better pick.

### What does Klaviyo's SMS bundle include that Mailchimp's does not?
Klaviyo Email + SMS at **$60/mo includes 1,000 SMS messages/month** with overage at $0.01-$0.02/message, all from one platform with email + SMS in the same flows. Mailchimp charges for SMS **separately per message** with no bundled SMS allowance in any tier — Mailchimp SMS starts at around $0.01-0.03/message plus a $20-30/mo platform fee. For stores sending both email and SMS at scale, Klaviyo's bundled SMS pricing is meaningfully cheaper and operationally simpler than Mailchimp's a la carte approach.
