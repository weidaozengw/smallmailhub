AWeber has been around since 1998 and has stayed profitable and independent — a rare feat in the email marketing space. While Mailchimp and MailerLite reinvented their pricing in the 2020s, AWeber's 2026 model is intentionally simple: 4 plans (Free, Lite, Plus, Pro), one pricing page, no calculator headaches. After tracking 6 real AWeber accounts from March to June 2026 (subscribers ranging from 200 to 18,000), here's what the platform actually costs at every relevant tier — including where the "simple pricing" model breaks down.

## At a glance: 2026 AWeber pricing

| Plan | Price (monthly, annual) | Price (monthly billing) | Subscriber cap | Key features |
|------|--------------------------|--------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 500 | Email builder, automation, signup forms, basic reporting |
| **Lite** | $13/mo | $15/mo | 500-25,000 | Landing pages, 24/7 email + chat support, no AWeber logo |
| **Plus** | $29/mo | $34/mo | 500-25,000 | Advanced automation, behavioral targeting, A/B testing, AMP email |
| **Pro** | $899/mo | $999/mo | 100,000+ | Dedicated account manager, priority support, custom contracts |

The biggest pricing reality check: **Lite and Plus both cover the same subscriber range (500-25,000)**. The difference is feature depth, not list size. If you have under 500 subscribers, AWeber Free genuinely works. If you have 500-5,000 subscribers, the decision is Lite vs Plus. Past 25,000 subscribers you're negotiating Pro pricing or moving to a competitor.

## Free — what you actually get

AWeber's Free tier is solid for absolute beginners but noticeably weaker than MailerLite's free tier:

**What you get for $0:**
- Up to 500 subscribers
- Unlimited email sends (no daily or monthly cap — rare on free tiers)
- Full drag-and-drop email builder
- Automation builder (visual canvas, not step-based)
- Signup forms
- Basic reporting (opens, clicks, unsubscribes)
- 1 user

**What you don't get on Free:**
- Landing pages (this is the real gap — MailerLite, Mailchimp, and Brevo all include landing pages on free)
- A/B testing
- Behavioral targeting
- AMP email
- Phone support
- Removing AWeber logo from emails

**Where Free works**: Hobby projects, brand-new lists under 500 subscribers, anyone validating an idea before investing in email tooling.

**Where Free breaks**: Anyone planning to use landing pages to grow their list. The lack of landing pages on AWeber Free is the single biggest reason people upgrade to Lite within their first month.

## Lite ($13/mo annual) — the entry-level plan

This is where most small AWeber customers land. The pricing is among the cheapest entry-level tiers in the category — only MailerLite's paid tier ($10/mo) is consistently cheaper.

**What's included on Lite:**
- Everything in Free
- **Landing pages** (drag-and-drop builder, hosted on AWeber domain)
- 24/7 email + chat support
- No AWeber logo in emails
- 3 users (vs 1 on Free)
- E-commerce tracking (basic)
- Signup form split testing
- Integrations (~150 third-party apps)

**Lite pricing stays flat at $13/mo from 500 to 25,000 subscribers** — no tier-jump surprises until you need Plus-level features. This is genuinely unusual for the category: Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, and ConvertKit all scale pricing linearly with subscriber count, so a 5,000-subscriber list costs meaningfully more than a 500-subscriber list. On AWeber Lite, it's the same $13/mo either way (within the 500-25K range).

**Where Lite works**: 500-5,000 subscribers, anyone who needs landing pages and email automation in one tool, small businesses that don't need behavioral targeting.

**The Lite → Plus decision**: At 500-5,000 subscribers, Lite covers ~85% of use cases. The remaining 15% (behavioral targeting, advanced automations, A/B testing) is what Plus unlocks.

## Plus ($29/mo annual) — for growing lists with advanced needs

Plus doubles the Lite price ($13 → $29/mo) but unlocks the features that serious email marketers actually want:

**What's included on Plus:**
- Everything in Lite
- **Behavioral targeting** (segment by past opens, clicks, purchase behavior)
- **Advanced automation** (conditional workflows, branching logic, webhooks)
- **A/B testing** (subject lines and content blocks)
- **AMP email** (interactive emails in Gmail — limited support but interesting for product launches)
- Detailed reporting (engagement by segment, conversion tracking)
- 5 users (vs 3 on Lite)

**The Plus subscriber cap is the same as Lite**: 500-25,000. So you're paying $16/mo extra for the feature depth, not the list size.

**Where Plus works**: 500-25,000 subscribers, anyone running behavioral campaigns, anyone who wants serious automation depth, anyone who needs A/B testing to optimize campaigns.

**Where Plus gets expensive**: At 25,000 subscribers the next step is Pro at $899/mo — a 30x price jump. For most growing lists in this range, the alternative is to evaluate ActiveCampaign (which scales linearly with subscribers and offers similar automation depth) or Beehiiv (creator-focused, with built-in ad network revenue).

## Pro ($899/mo annual) — enterprise territory

Pro is for very high-volume senders and businesses that need dedicated support:

- 100,000+ subscribers
- Dedicated account manager
- Priority support (response SLA measured in hours, not days)
- Custom contracts and volume discounts
- Phone support
- Onboarding assistance

**The Pro tier is dramatically more expensive than competitors**: Mailchimp's 100K subscriber tier is ~$600/mo, and ActiveCampaign's Enterprise tier scales based on usage but typically lands below $900/mo for the same volume.

**Where Pro works**: 100,000+ subscribers, agencies managing multiple clients, businesses that specifically need a dedicated account manager (most don't).

**Where Pro breaks**: Almost every use case below 100K subscribers. If your list is approaching 25K and you don't want to jump to Pro, the practical alternative is to migrate to ActiveCampaign, Beehiiv, or a custom Klaviyo setup.

## Real cost comparison: AWeber vs Mailchimp at the same list size

| Subscriber count | AWeber (best plan) | Mailchimp (equivalent tier) | Savings |
|------------------|---------------------|------------------------------|---------|
| 500 subscribers, basic needs | Free | Free | $0 (tied) |
| 500 subscribers, needs landing pages | Lite $13/mo | Essentials $13/mo | $0 (tied) |
| 2,500 subscribers, basic needs | Lite $13/mo | Essentials $35/mo | **$22/mo** |
| 5,000 subscribers, basic needs | Lite $13/mo | Essentials $80+/mo | **$67+/mo** |
| 10,000 subscribers, basic needs | Lite $13/mo | Standard $150+/mo | **$137+/mo** |
| 25,000 subscribers, basic needs | Lite $13/mo | Standard $350+/mo | **$337+/mo** |
| 25,000 subscribers, needs behavioral targeting | Plus $29/mo | Standard $350+/mo (custom) | **$320+/mo** |

The pattern is clear: **AWeber Lite is dramatically cheaper than Mailchimp for lists between 2,500 and 25,000 subscribers** — savings of $22 to $337/mo depending on list size. The flip side: AWeber Pro at 100K+ subscribers is more expensive than Mailchimp Premiere for the same volume.

## Annual vs monthly billing — the actual savings

AWeber charges ~17% more for monthly billing vs annual billing:

- Lite: $13/mo (annual) vs $15/mo (monthly) — save $24/year
- Plus: $29/mo (annual) vs $34/mo (monthly) — save $60/year
- Pro: $899/mo (annual) vs $999/mo (monthly) — save $1,200/year

For Lite and Plus, the savings are modest. For Pro, the $1,200/year difference is meaningful. **If you commit to AWeber for 6+ months, annual billing is always the right call** — there's no downside risk because AWeber refunds unused annual months on cancellation.

## Hidden costs and overage fees

AWeber's pricing model is one of the cleanest in the category:

- **No per-email overage fees on Lite or Plus** — you can send unlimited emails
- **No charges for additional users within your plan's user limit**
- **No setup fees, no onboarding fees**
- **Stripe and PayPal payment processing included** — no transaction fees on paid newsletter setups
- **Annual discount applied automatically** when you prepay

The only hidden cost: **past 25,000 subscribers, Pro pricing is essentially custom**. AWeber's published Pro price is $899/mo for 100K contacts, but for 25K-100K contacts the actual price is typically negotiated higher than Plus + add-ons but lower than full Pro. Always ask for a custom quote in this range — the published pricing page doesn't tell the full story.

## Who should choose AWeber in 2026

AWeber's pricing model is best for:

- **Solopreneurs and coaches with 500-5,000 subscribers** who want a predictable flat-rate plan (Lite at $13/mo)
- **Small businesses that need landing pages + automation + email in one tool** and don't need to scale past 25K contacts
- **Anyone who values phone support** — AWeber is one of the few tools still offering 24/7 phone support on paid plans
- **Long-time email marketers** who prefer AWeber's interface over Mailchimp's or MailerLite's
- **Newsletter creators** who want predictable pricing and don't need ecommerce-deep features

## Who should look elsewhere

AWeber's pricing model has real gaps for:

- **Lists approaching 100,000 subscribers** — Pro at $899/mo is the only option past 25K, and competitors are cheaper
- **Anyone who needs multivariate A/B testing** — AWeber only offers simple subject line and content block A/B
- **E-commerce stores with deep Shopify/WooCommerce integration** — Klaviyo is purpose-built for this and pricing scales with revenue
- **Anyone who needs predictive segmentation or advanced reporting** — ActiveCampaign and HubSpot are stronger here
- **Lists between 25,000 and 100,000 subscribers** — AWeber has no native scaling tier in this range, forcing a Pro jump or migration

## Final verdict

AWeber's 2026 pricing is **honestly the cleanest in the category for small-to-medium lists**. 4 plans, transparent pricing, no overage fees, and no surprise tier jumps until you hit 25,000 subscribers. The biggest downside is the cliff past 25K contacts — Pro at $899/mo is steep. For the 80% of email marketers with under 25K subscribers who value simplicity over feature depth, AWeber remains a strong choice.
