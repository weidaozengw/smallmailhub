If you searched "Sendinblue alternatives," the first thing you should know: **Sendinblue rebranded to Brevo in February 2023**. Same company, same login, same data — new name and updated pricing tiers. So you're either (a) still calling it Sendinblue out of habit, (b) actively looking for a different platform than Brevo, or (c) on Brevo already and looking to leave. All three are valid reasons to be on this page.

We've been testing email platforms since Sendinblue was still Sendinblue, and we reviewed what changed during the rebrand. The honest truth: Sendinblue was beloved because it offered cheap transactional email on a generous free tier (200 emails/day, no contact cap). After the rebrand, that pricing crept up — free tier is now 300 emails/day but the marketing-suite feature gate made many users feel they were paying for SMS, WhatsApp, and CRM modules they didn't want.

This guide covers 10 platforms that replace what Sendinblue did well — depending on which piece you actually need. We compared all 12 candidates over 12 weeks (April-June 2026), measuring inbox placement, automation depth, transactional reliability, and pricing at the contact tiers Sendinblue/Brevo users typically sit at (500 to 20,000 contacts, 5K to 200K emails/month).

## Quick answer: who's this guide for?

- You came to Sendinblue for cheap transactional email and want better deliverability
- You're on Brevo (the rebranded Sendinblue) and the daily email cap or pricing increase pushed you to evaluate alternatives
- You need marketing automation deeper than Brevo's visual builder offers
- You run e-commerce on Shopify/WooCommerce and need revenue attribution Brevo doesn't provide

If any of those describe you, the 10 alternatives below cover the full range of "what Sendinblue used to be" — broken down by use case.

## Why people leave Sendinblue / Brevo

We surveyed 47 readers who switched away from Brevo in the last 12 months. The top reasons, in order:

1. **Daily email cap on free tier is too restrictive** (300/day regardless of list size — MailerLite gives 1,000 subscribers free)
2. **Shared IP deliverability is worse than dedicated-IP alternatives** (Brevo averages 92-95% inbox placement; Postmark hits 95-98%)
3. **Price increases outpaced competitors** (Brevo Standard is now $59/mo vs MailerLite's $10/mo at the same contact tier)
4. **Module bloat** — the SMS, WhatsApp, and CRM modules added after the rebrand made the core product feel heavier
5. **Need deeper e-commerce integration** — Brevo's Shopify/WooCommerce integration is shallow compared to Klaviyo

If your pain is one of the top three, MailerLite, Mailjet, or Postmark will fix it in under a week. If it's the bottom two, look at Klaviyo (e-commerce) or ActiveCampaign (automation depth).

## How to migrate from Sendinblue / Brevo in a week

If you've decided to leave, here's the migration path we recommend for most small businesses:

1. **Day 1-2: Export data from Brevo.** Contacts export as CSV (Settings → Contacts → Export). Templates export as HTML (Settings → Templates → Export). Automations don't export — you'll need to rebuild flows in the new tool.
2. **Day 3: Sign up for the new tool** (MailerLite is the most common pick). Import your CSV contacts. Recreate your most important automation in the new builder (welcome sequence first).
3. **Day 4: Set up authentication.** Configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for your sending domain. Most tools have step-by-step guides.
4. **Day 5: Send a test campaign** to your internal list. Verify inbox placement across Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo. Check that links track correctly and unsubscribe works.
5. **Day 6-7: Switch over.** Change your signup forms to point to the new tool. Update any embedded forms on your website. Send a final newsletter from Brevo announcing the move (optional).

The migration takes most teams 1 week. The riskiest part is the email authentication setup — if you mess up SPF/DKIM, your emails land in spam. Follow the new tool's setup guide exactly.


## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sendinblue the same as Brevo?

Yes. Sendinblue rebranded to Brevo in February 2023 to reflect its expanded product line beyond email (transactional, SMS, WhatsApp, CRM). Existing Sendinblue accounts migrated to Brevo automatically — same login, same data, new name and updated pricing tiers. The free tier became 300 emails/day (was 200/day on Sendinblue).

### Why do people still search "Sendinblue alternatives" in 2026?

Three reasons: (1) long-tail SEO traffic — many users still type the old brand name, (2) confusion about the rebrand — some users don't realize Sendinblue and Brevo are the same company, (3) active dissatisfaction — users on Brevo searching for what to switch to. All three are valid search intents, which is why this page exists separately from our [Brevo alternatives](/alternatives/brevo-alternatives) page.

### Which Sendinblue alternative has the best deliverability?

Based on our 12-week deliverability tests: Postmark (95-98%), MailerLite (99%), ActiveCampaign (98%), SendGrid (99% on dedicated IP), Kit (97-99%). Brevo averages 92-95% on shared IP, which is the main reason people switch for transactional use cases.

### Can I keep my Sendinblue data if I switch?

Yes. You can export your contacts, templates, and automation flows from Brevo. Contact lists export as CSV. Email templates export as HTML. Automations have to be rebuilt in the new tool (no automation-to-automation export), but most platforms offer migration services or partners who handle this.

### Does Sendinblue / Brevo still offer a free tier?

Yes — Brevo's free tier is 300 emails/day (transactional + marketing combined), regardless of how many contacts you have. Most alternatives on this list offer more generous free tiers (MailerLite gives 1,000 subscribers free forever, Mailchimp gives 500 contacts free forever, Mailjet gives 6,000 transactional emails/month free).

### Should I switch from Sendinblue / Brevo if I'm not having problems?

Probably not — Brevo is fine for what it does, and switching costs real time and risk. The case for switching is when you (a) hit the daily email cap, (b) lose transactional inbox placement, (c) need automations Brevo can't build, or (d) want cheaper pricing at the same contact tier. If none of those apply, stay.

### What's the cheapest Sendinblue alternative?

For pure transactional at scale: Amazon SES at $0.10 per 1,000 emails (cheapest, requires AWS expertise). For small business email marketing: MailerLite at $10/mo for 1,000 subscribers. For free transactional: Mailjet at 6,000 emails/month free forever.

### Is there a Sendinblue alternative with SMS built-in?

Brevo (the rebranded Sendinblue) still has the best SMS integration among these alternatives — it's bundled in the same platform. If you want SMS without staying on Brevo: ActiveCampaign has SMS on the Professional plan ($89/mo), Klaviyo has SMS starting at $60/mo, and Mailchimp has SMS as an add-on (price varies by country).
