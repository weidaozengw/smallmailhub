Email marketing tools are a strong affiliate niche because they have **high customer lifetime value**, **predictable monthly recurring revenue**, and **clear product-market fit**. But not all ESP affiliate programs pay the same. We compared 11 email marketing tool affiliate programs across four dimensions that actually matter to affiliates: **commission rate**, **cookie window**, **payout threshold**, and **tool quality** (because a bad product means high churn means lost recurring revenue).

After reviewing each program's public affiliate page, partner dashboard, and published payout terms, here's the honest ranking of the seven strongest ESP affiliate programs in 2026.

## How we compared these affiliate programs

For each ESP, we verified the following from the official affiliate/partner page:

- **Commission structure** — recurring (12 months, 24 months, or lifetime) vs one-time
- **Commission rate** — 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, or hybrid
- **Cookie window** — how long after the click you get credit for the sale (30, 60, 90 days)
- **Payout threshold** — minimum balance before you can withdraw ($30, $50, $100)
- **Payout method** — PayPal, bank transfer, store credit
- **Real-world conversion** — does the tool actually convert trial users to paid customers (churn matters more than signup)
- **Average customer lifetime** — how long a referred customer stays subscribed (months)
- **Affiliates terms clarity** — public, predictable, no surprise clawbacks

We weighted commission rate and cookie window the heaviest because they directly affect earnings per click.

## Quick answer: which ESP affiliate program is best for which scenario?

- **Highest recurring commission**: ConvertKit (Kit) and Beehiiv — 50% for 12 months
- **Best lifetime commission**: ActiveCampaign — 30% recurring for life
- **Lowest payout threshold**: AWeber — $30 minimum withdrawal
- **Longest cookie window**: ConvertKit, Beehiiv, GetResponse, AWeber, Brevo — all 90 days
- **Best hybrid commission**: Brevo — $5 one-time + 30% recurring
- **Best for newsletter-affiliate creators**: Beehiiv (built-in ad network on top of affiliate)
- **Best for high-volume affiliates**: AWeber ($30 payout means faster cash flow)
- **Best for premium-affiliate positioning**: ActiveCampaign (higher customer LTV, higher commission dollars per signup)

If you want a one-line answer: **ConvertKit (Kit) at 50% recurring for 12 months is the default best affiliate program for most affiliates**, with Beehiiv as the creator-focused alternative and ActiveCampaign as the lifetime-commission pick.

## The 7 best email marketing tool affiliate programs in 2026

**Affiliate program**: [Kit Affiliate Program](https://kit.com/affiliates)

- **Cookie window**: 90 days
- **Payout threshold**: $50

- **Average customer LTV**: 14-22 months (creator-focused, low churn)
- **Conversion rate**: ~3-5% trial-to-paid (industry average: 2-3%)

Kit's affiliate program is the strongest in the category for three reasons: **50% recurring for 12 months** is the highest standard rate, the **90-day cookie** gives your content time to convert, and Kit's **creator-focused positioning** means referred customers stay longer (creators don't switch ESPs often — once their automations are built, churn is low).

**What we liked**:
- 50% recurring is the highest standard rate in the ESP category
- 90-day cookie window (longer than ActiveCampaign's 60 days, MailerLite's 60 days)
- Kit's customer base (creators) has low churn — better LTV for affiliates

- $50 payout threshold is reasonable for mid-volume affiliates
- Affiliate links work across all Kit landing pages

**What we didn't**:

- 50% drops to 0% after 12 months — no lifetime commission option

- No tiered commission rates for high-volume affiliates

**Best for**: Content creators promoting email tools as a secondary revenue stream, comparison-site operators, newsletter sponsors.

**Bottom line**: If you're promoting one ESP and want the highest standard recurring commission in the category, **Kit is the default choice**. The 50% recurring for 12 months + 90-day cookie combination is hard to beat.

## How to choose the right affiliate program for your traffic

The right ESP affiliate program depends on your audience and content type:

**Promoting to creators / bloggers / course sellers**: ConvertKit (Kit) is the default choice. Beehiiv is the fastest-growing alternative.

**Promoting to traditional small businesses / coaches / consultants**: AWeber (mature audience), GetResponse (mid-tier), or ActiveCampaign (B2B).

**Promoting to budget-conscious beginners / hobbyists**: MailerLite. The lower price point converts better, even at 30% commission.

**Promoting to e-commerce / SaaS / B2B**: ActiveCampaign (lifetime commission + B2B LTV) or Brevo (transactional email positioning + hybrid commission).

**Want fast cash flow**: AWeber ($30 payout threshold) or Brevo ($5 per signup guaranteed).

**Want long-term revenue**: ActiveCampaign (30% for life).

**Want highest standard rate**: ConvertKit or Beehiiv (50% recurring for 12 months).

## Common affiliate program pitfalls to avoid

- **Stacking affiliate programs against each other**: Most ESP affiliate terms don't allow you to promote a competitor in the same content piece without clear disclosure. Read the terms carefully.
- **Promoting to the wrong audience**: Promoting Kit to enterprise B2B buyers won't convert. Match the affiliate program to your audience.
- **Ignoring cookie window differences**: A 60-day cookie (ActiveCampaign, MailerLite) means you lose credit if the user doesn't convert within 2 months. A 90-day cookie gives your content more time to convert.
- **Chasing high commission rates over conversion**: 50% recurring on a tool that doesn't convert is worse than 30% on a tool that does. MailerLite at 30% with 4-6% conversion often beats Kit at 50% with 3-5% conversion for some audiences.
- **Forgetting to disclose affiliate relationships**: FTC requires clear disclosure (#affiliate, "I earn a commission", etc.). All major ESP affiliate programs also require disclosure in their terms.
