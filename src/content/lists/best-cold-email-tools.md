Cold email is fundamentally different from email marketing. You're sending personalized outreach to people who've never opted in — so you need deliverability tools, automated follow-up sequences, and lead database integration. Standard email marketing tools (Mailchimp, MailerLite) will burn your domain within weeks if used for cold outreach.

We compared 7 cold email platforms on published pricing, documentation and feature depth over six months.

## What to look for in a cold email tool

**Must-have features**:
- Email warmup (essential for deliverability)
- Automated follow-up sequences (4-7 touches per prospect)
- Personalization variables ({{first_name}}, {{company}})
- Reply detection (stop sequence when prospect replies)
- Bounce handling (auto-pause sequences on hard bounces)
- Lead database or integration (Apollo, ZoomInfo, etc.)

**Nice-to-have features**:
- Multi-channel sequences (email + LinkedIn)
- AI reply categorization
- A/B testing subject lines
- Inbox rotation across multiple accounts
- CRM integration (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)

## Cold email setup mistakes to avoid

1. **Sending without warmup** — your domain will be blacklisted within 2 weeks. Always run warmup for 2-4 weeks before sending real campaigns.
2. **Sending from your primary domain** — use a separate domain (yourcompany.co instead of yourcompany.com) for cold outreach. Protects your main brand.
3. **Too many emails per day** — start with 20/day per account, scale to 50/day after 2 weeks. Google/Microsoft limit new accounts aggressively.
4. **No follow-ups** — 60% of replies come from follow-up emails. Build 4-7 touch sequences.
5. **Ignoring reply intent** — positive replies should pause the sequence immediately. Most tools do this, but verify before sending.

## Cold email vs email marketing tools

Don't use Mailchimp, MailerLite, or Kit for cold email. Reasons:
1. These tools don't have warmup features
2. They have low daily sending limits (designed for opt-in lists)
3. They report on opens/clicks (irrelevant for cold email)
4. Their domain reputation algorithms flag cold-style content

Use dedicated cold email tools instead. Cost is similar ($30-100/mo), but deliverability is dramatically better.
